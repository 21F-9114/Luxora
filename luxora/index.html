About Luxora

A house built on patience and gold

Luxora began in Multan with a simple idea: jewelry should be made to be kept, not replaced. Every piece that leaves our studio is designed, cast and finished by hand, using real gold and honestly sourced stones.

That standard is set and upheld by our CEO, Inaya Nadeem, whose vision for the brand shapes every collection — from the first sketch to the final polish.
